# Hung Pump · APP Arwana Codex Plugin Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-03

Hung Pump Industrial Co., Ltd. ("Hung Pump", "we") provides the Hung Pump · APP Arwana Codex Plugin (the "Plugin"). This policy explains how information is handled when the Plugin is used.

## 1. Scope

The Plugin is a skill-only package containing instructions and local brand assets. It does not include an independent App, MCP server, account system, analytics service, advertising SDK, or payment function.

## 2. Information processed

The Plugin may process the prompts, engineering parameters, files, and context that a user chooses to provide within Codex for pump selection, VFD control planning, system assembly planning, or market analysis. Processing occurs within the user's Codex environment and is also subject to the applicable OpenAI product terms and privacy policy.

## 3. Information collected by Hung Pump

The current Plugin does not independently transmit prompts, files, telemetry, or usage analytics to Hung Pump. Hung Pump receives information only when a user voluntarily contacts us, submits a support request, requests a quotation, or sends files through a separate communication channel.

## 4. Support communications

Information sent to support may include name, company, contact details, message content, and attachments. We use it to respond, diagnose issues, provide requested services, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Retention is limited to what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

## 5. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used for email, hosting, security, or business operations, subject to appropriate contractual and legal safeguards. Use of Codex may involve processing by OpenAI under its own terms.

## 6. Security and rights

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures. Depending on applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about their personal data.

## 7. Children

The Plugin is intended for business and engineering users and is not directed to children.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
